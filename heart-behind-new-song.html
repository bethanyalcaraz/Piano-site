The Heart

The Heart Behind New Song

The word “new” appears many times throughout Scripture.

New heart. New spirit. New creation. New life. New song! New heaven & earth…

It is so clear that God loves to do NEW things in our hearts & lives 🤍 and that new things bring glory & honor Him.

When I prayed about this worship program, I believe the Lord was putting on my heart the word “new.” I believe He wants to do a new thing, invite believers who by faith will learn a new skill, & equip them with a new way to worship Him in the secret place. And (if called!) grow to become new worship leaders that will lead the church in instrument & song. So my heart is not only to teach how to play the instrument, but to encourage students to seek Him & know Him more through declaring truth with music.

“But the hour is coming, and now is, when the true worshipers will worship the Father in spirit and truth; for the Father is seeking such to worship Him. God is Spirit, and those who worship Him must worship in spirit and truth.” John 4:23-24 NKJV
