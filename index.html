The Launch Program

Every new student begins with the Launch Program — six lessons for $250 — before moving into regular lessons. Here's what it includes:

What You'll Learn

  • Sit down and play worship music all on your own
  • Learn the skill sets needed for playing with a worship team
  • Play common worship piano chords and chord progressions confidently
  • Play simple left-hand patterns and right-hand melodies together
  • Play along with worship recordings and live arrangements
  • Grow in confidence, musicianship, and servant-hearted worship leading

Every Lesson Includes

I.

A Devo to Start

Each lesson opens with a short devotional, grounding your playing in worship before you touch the keys.

II.

Real Worship Skills

Chords, chord progressions, and color chords — plus how to flow through worship music in different ways — so you can play confidently on your own.

III.

Your Worship Portfolio

Build a growing personal songbook of chords and skills you keep and use long after the lesson ends.

About Me

Hi, I'm Bethany

My husband, Philip, and I live in Vista, CA, where we both serve at Calvary Vista. Philip is on staff as the Junior High pastor, and I have the joy of playing keys on the worship team.

Serving the Lord through worship and being part of worship teams has been one of the greatest blessings in my life. Since 2017, I have taught piano lessons to well over 100 students, with a focus on both classical piano and worship music. My heart is not only to teach students the joy of making music, but also to cultivate a heart of worship through discipleship and by encouraging their relationship with Jesus.

I am excited to be reopening my piano studio this summer, beginning with a 6-week Summer Worship Piano Program. Following the program, I will continue offering private lessons for students interested in learning worship piano.

One-Time Enrollment Fee

A one-time $50 enrollment fee reserves your spot in the Launch Program and covers all supplies. There are no books or other materials to purchase — everything you need for lessons is included. This fee is waived for students who already have worship music experience.

The Launch Program must be completed before enrolling in a monthly package. Subscription packages are billed monthly. Rates are placeholders and subject to change. Prices above are for in-studio lessons. Students may inquire about in-home lessons, which include an additional travel fee: $25 for two lessons, $50 for four lessons.
