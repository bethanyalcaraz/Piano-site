Meet Miss Bethany

Hi, I'm Bethany! I can't wait to share my love of the piano with you.

I started playing piano at age 5, when my two best friends started lessons. They didn't stick with it — but I did!

Today I'm a classically trained pianist, and I've been teaching piano since 2017 — well over 100 students so far! In lessons, we build the same strong foundations I was trained in: good technique and confident music reading. But my favorite part? Teaching improvisation and chord theory, and watching students light up when they realize they can create music of their own.

When I'm not teaching, you'll find me at Calvary Vista, where I play keys on the worship team and my husband, Philip, serves as the Junior High pastor. Vista is home for us, and I reopened my studio here in 2026 — I'd love to meet your family!

I'm excited to introduce you to a love of music, and hold your hand as we take this journey one step at a time!

What students learn

I

Technique

Healthy posture, hand position, scales, and arpeggios that build strong, relaxed, expressive playing.

II

Reading & Theory

Fluent note reading, rhythm, and the theory behind the music — so students understand what they play.

III

Improvisation & Chords

Chord theory, chord progressions, and improvisation foundations — so students can create music, not just read it.

IV

Musicianship

Dynamics, phrasing, and expression that turn notes on a page into real music — plus a personal portfolio of songs they love.

How tuition works

I

Your reserved spot

Each family reserves a day of the week and a time. That spot is yours all year — the same lesson time, every week.

II

Same tuition every month

Tuition is the same each month — including shorter months like December and months with a fifth week.

III

School calendar

The studio follows the school calendar, so there are no lessons on school holidays. In months with a fifth week, there are no lessons that fifth week.

IV

Make-up lessons

If I need to cancel a lesson, it will always be made up. Make-ups for lessons a student misses may be offered when time allows, but aren't guaranteed.
